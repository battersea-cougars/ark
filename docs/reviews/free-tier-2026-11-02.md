# Load and free-tier review, 2 November 2026

Section 1 of the pre-launch review ([#148](https://github.com/battersea-cougars/ark/issues/148)), for the go-live
plan ([#181](https://github.com/battersea-cougars/ark/issues/181)): launch week's busy moments run against dev, and
each free-tier limit checked with Cloudflare's own numbers. On `main` at `884b33d`, with the fixes below on
`team/load-free-tier`. How the runs work: [load-testing.md](../load-testing.md); why: ADR
[0121](../adr/0121-load-and-concurrency.md), the limits: ADR [0003](../adr/0003-free-tiers-only.md), the Usage page
and its 70% / 90% check: ADR [0059](../adr/0059-usage-page-and-check.md).

**Where the numbers come from.** Dev's runs are Cloudflare's analytics for the run's window (the whole dev account,
crons included), on dev's club of 27 (anonymized) plus the run's 120 members. Local runs are a Worker of the run's own
under `wrangler dev`, 120 members, D1's own row counts; `--weeks 52` puts a year of training nights and four Kumites
behind the club first (`scripts/seed-history.mjs`), since production starts empty and grows. **Rows read are what D1
bills and the line to watch**; local latency and CPU are this machine's.

## The short of it

- **Fixed: every sign-up worked out the whole club's charges again.** After a year of nights that's ~50,000 rows a
  tap, and a Friday's sign-ups alone would read 5.7M rows, 114% of the free day. A sign-up now does it only on the
  event's day (when it can be charged): 356 rows a tap, the rush 0.65M (13%).
- **Fixed: the minute push drain could read the whole outbox every minute** to find nothing to prune (a week of
  notifications, 1,440 times a day). It now finds the old ones by index.
- **A Kumite draft night is the dearest thing the club does: 63% of the free day on dev**, and Cloudflare stopped 11
  of its requests for CPU. Workers' CPU is over the free plan's 10 ms at the median on Cloudflare (#225), and the
  draft's reads are #162 (after launch today).
- **Filed for launch:** the register and the hourly run still work out every charge ever (#222: 52k rows a mark after
  a year); each register mark makes every open phone read the whole club (#223); the after-party's reveals pass the
  per-address limit on one wifi (#224); Workers CPU (#225).
- Requests, rows written, the live hub, storage, the crons, Sanity, Gmail and FCM have plenty of room.

## The every-minute drain, idle

The minute cron (`index.ts scheduled`) does nothing but the push drain, and from :12 a claim on the hour's catch-up.
Every statement it sends, with its plan (`EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN`, `push.drain-reads.use-cases.test.ts`):

- **Prune** `DELETE FROM push_outbox WHERE id IN (SELECT id … WHERE created_at < ? ORDER BY id LIMIT ?)`: SQLite chose
  to walk the outbox in id order (no sort) and test each row's date: every row of the week, every minute, to find
  none. **Fixed:** ordered by `created_at, id`, it's a range on `push_outbox_created`: only the rows it deletes.
  On dev D1's planner had already chosen the index (2,190 rows read an hour idle, ~36 a run, the outbox 118 rows);
  without statistics it doesn't, and production's outbox (each member's notices for a week) is the larger table.
- `push_told` pruned by `push_told_at`; what's due dropped and claimed by `push_outbox_due` (state, then the time):
  nothing due reads nothing.
- The hour's claim: one row (`hourly_run`).

Dev, idle hours: about 72 invocations an hour, 2,190 rows read and 4 written, so **~52k rows read a day (1%) and ~100
written** from the crons. The five-minute run adds the sign-up announcements (`training_series` and `tournaments`
scanned: both small, tens of rows a year) and the website and dues reminder checks. The hourly run's recharge is the
expensive one as history grows (#222).

## What changed

- `apps/team/worker/entries/entries.routes.ts`, `entries.ts`, `api/api.ts`: a sign-up names `charges` (and so works
  the charges out again) only when its event's day has come (`chargedFromToday`); `api.ts` recharges for a change a
  handler adds (`alsoChanged`) as for one a route declares. Test: `entries/entries.charges.use-cases.test.ts` (failed
  before: Tuesday's sign-up for Friday worked the charges out).
- `apps/team/worker/push/push.ts`: the drain's prune in `created_at` order. Test:
  `push/push.drain-reads.use-cases.test.ts` (failed before: `SCAN push_outbox`).
- `scripts/load`: three new runs, `register`, `reveals` and `club-send` (each takes `--followers`), and `--weeks N` for
  a Worker of the run's own, a past behind the club (`seed-history`). `club-send` registers its phones with tokens
  Firebase has for nobody (it answers "unregistered", so the phone is forgotten and nothing reaches anyone), and only
  the run's own members are told (its own night's teams), never dev's real ones.
